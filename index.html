Welcome to the clinic

Specialist knowledge. A considered approach.

The London Dermatology Centre is a private dermatology clinic founded by Dr Sunil Chopra in 2005.

The clinic provides medical, surgical and aesthetic dermatology for adults and children. Its consultant-led team assesses a broad range of skin, hair and nail concerns, helping patients understand their options and make informed decisions about care.

Located on Wimpole Street, the centre brings specialist dermatology services together in a central London setting.

A connected care experience

Understand your skin. Explore your options.

A dermatology consultation is an opportunity to discuss your concerns, review relevant history and agree on an appropriate plan with your clinician.

  • Time to discuss your symptoms, concerns and treatment goals.
  • Clinical assessment and diagnostic investigations when needed.
  • Discussion of suitable medical, surgical or aesthetic options.
  • Follow-up arrangements based on your individual care plan.

Assessment and investigations

When a closer look is needed.

Depending on your symptoms and clinical assessment, your clinician may recommend further investigations to help understand your skin concern.

The clinic offers diagnostic services including selected blood tests, swabs, allergy tests, patch testing and biopsies. Your clinician will explain which tests are relevant and what they involve.
