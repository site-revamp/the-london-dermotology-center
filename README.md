# The London Dermatology Centre Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

The London Dermatology Centre is a private, consultant-led dermatology clinic based on Wimpole Street, London. Founded in 2005 by Dr Sunil Chopra, the clinic provides medical, surgical, paediatric and aesthetic dermatology services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on strengthening brand authenticity, improving the relevance of visual content and creating clearer navigation to specialist treatment information.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key problems identified during the website review.

### 1. Replacement of the Placeholder Logo
- Identified the circular letter "L" as an inaccurate representation of the clinic's brand identity.
- Replaced the placeholder with the official clinic logo.
- Improved brand consistency and recognition throughout the homepage.

### 2. More Relevant and Authentic Photography
- Addressed the use of generic stock photography that did not establish the clinic's identity.
- Improved the visual direction with imagery more relevant to specialist dermatology and clinical services.
- Focused on creating a stronger connection between the visual presentation and the clinic's professional positioning.

### 3. Improved Service Navigation and Internal Linking
- Addressed treatment links that incorrectly redirected visitors to the homepage.
- Improved navigation pathways to relevant treatment information.
- Strengthened the internal linking structure to support service discovery, usability and SEO.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Brand identity:** Improved logo placement and brand consistency through the use of the official clinic identity.
- **Visual presentation:** Focused on more relevant imagery aligned with dermatology and specialist healthcare.
- **Layout:** Strengthened the organisation of homepage content and service navigation.
- **User experience:** Made treatment information easier to discover through more relevant navigation pathways.
- **Consistency:** Aligned visual elements and content presentation with the clinic's specialist positioning.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more authentic and professional homepage experience that better represents the London Dermatology Centre.
